Meet the Designer

Jennifer Cooper

This is Jennifer, a 30-something mom of two living in Virginia with her husband, who she considers her best friend. She's a proud Swiftie, a book lover, and someone who cares deeply about human rights and living life with intention and heart.

Jennifer has always been the creative type. From sketching, writing, photography, and constantly chasing the feeling of turning an idea into something real. Once becoming a homeowner, interior design became a natural love story for her. She became fully obsessed with making spaces feel personal, beautiful, and truly her without breaking the budget.

Before long, friends and family started coming to her for design help, and her fixation became a passion. She loved the opportunity to turn "blah" spaces into warm, character-filled rooms - spaces that truly reflected the people living in them.

While she was often encouraged to pursue in-home interior design professionally, it never felt quite like the right fit. Traditional design can feel out of reach for many people, and she wanted to change that. That's where JC Interiors comes in. Making home design fun, accessible, and flexible to help ANYONE create a space they actually love on their time, their style, and their budget.
